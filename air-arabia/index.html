Air Arabia

Campaign · Escape the ordinary, Where next?

ClientAir Arabia
CampaignEscape the Ordinary
ScopeKey visuals, OOH, DOOH, social
RoleConcept & art direction

Half the office is already somewhere else.

A value carrier that speaks in prices gets compared on prices. For Gulf professionals the escape is a version of themselves they put on hold between Sunday and Thursday. The campaign makes that place visible: the person stays composed, what they want shows through the fabric.

02 · Craft

Up close
