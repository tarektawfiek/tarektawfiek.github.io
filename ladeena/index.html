Ladeena

Rebranding · The art of self-care · لدينة

ClientLadeena
ProjectRebranding proposal
ScopeWordmark, type, colour, scents guide, social, packaging
RoleArt direction

Same pink. Less ornament.

Ladeena had a loyal following and a busy logo. The rebrand re-introduces her as feminine, unique and alluring: simpler, more modern and more luxurious, while keeping the pink everyone knows and the star as her sign of elegance.

01 · Re-brand

Keep the star, lose the frame

The ornamental badge gives way to a slim serif wordmark with a light Arabic logotype beneath it. Only the star survives, now a small spark beside the name.

→
02 · Typography

Soft serif, two scripts

A high-contrast serif for headlines and a clean geometric sans for everything else, matched in Arabic so both languages speak in the same voice.

03 · Colour

Same pink, brighter maroon

The pink everyone knows stays, from tea rose to soft peach, sharpened by a bright maroon for contrast and confidence.

Bright maroon#C0254F

04 · The name

Five letters, five scents

لدينة is the woman with soft, delicate skin. Each letter of her name became a trait, and each trait a scent family.

للطافةFloral
ددلالSweet
ييفوعFresh
ننعومةPowdery
ةتألّقLuxurious · Woody
05 · Scents guide

Find your scent by colour

Every product sorted into its family and colour-coded, from the chart to the label, and the gift box.

06 · Mood

Soft light, real skin

Linen, petals, bare shoulders and warm pink light: a photography mood that sells the feeling before the product.

07 · Social

Pink, from post to post

Product-led posts in the new pink, accented in maroon, each one named after its scent family, with educational posts that explain the brand.

08 · Packaging

Gifts, not boxes

Bags, sachets, drawer boxes and swing tags in tea rose and maroon, finished with the wordmark and nothing else.
