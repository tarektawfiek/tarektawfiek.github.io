Kohler × INDEX

Photography & video direction · INDEX Saudi Arabia 2024

ClientKohler
EventINDEX Saudi Arabia, Riyadh
ScopePhotography moodboard, video direction, content style
RoleArt direction

Shoot the booth like a showroom.

Trade-fair coverage usually looks like a trade fair: harsh halls, crowded frames, products lost in the noise. For Kohler's presence at INDEX, the Kingdom's leading interior design and fit-out event, the direction was the opposite: every frame should feel as calm, crafted and premium as a Kohler bathroom.

01 · The stage

Where the Kingdom shops for design

INDEX gathers international and local brands shaping interiors in Saudi Arabia. The booth had to stand out in that mosaic, and so did the content made inside it.

02 · Content style

Modern, sophisticated, clean

Two rules for everything we shot: keep Kohler's premium, innovative identity in front, and let the craftsmanship and materials do the talking.

Look & feel

Modern, sophisticated and clean. Fewer elements per frame, more space around the product.

Brand tone

Elegance and luxury, with craftsmanship and high-quality materials as the hero of every shot.

03 · Angles & composition

Wide for the space, close for the craft

Wide angles to capture the whole booth, tight frames for textures and finishes, and hero products shot low looking up or high looking down for a sense of grandeur.

Wide: the full boothTight: finish & textureHero: low or high angle
04 · Audience

Real interest, caught candidly

Visitors touching the products and talking to the team, shot naturally and never posed, so the content proves the booth worked.

05 · Motion

Energy in the aisles

Slow motion and time-lapse of people moving through the booth and around the products, adding life between the still, crafted frames.

06 · Light & colour

Soft light, signature metals

Soft, diffused light with no harsh shadows, natural where possible and precise artificial light to bring out each finish. Neutral tones, lifted by Kohler's brushed nickel and matte black.
