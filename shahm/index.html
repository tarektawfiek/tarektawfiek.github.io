The brief: boutique luxury.

Shahm asked for a boutique, luxurious brand, the opposite of a construction company. No hard hats, no cranes, no heavy industrial type. An identity that feels as considered as the palaces and private villas it builds, for clients who notice every detail.

A name that already carries weight.

شهم means noble, gallant, principled: someone you trust with the most important things. Shahm builds palaces, mosques, schools and plants for clients who notice everything, so the brand had to feel like a trusted custodian, never a construction company. No hardhats, no cranes, no raw industrial imagery.

01 · Direction

Royal luxury

الفخامة الملكية. Warm light, limestone, linen and space: luxury that is felt before it is announced.

02 · Concept

Three letters, one signature

S, H and M interlock into a single monogram: long upright strokes meeting fine curves, precise in its detail and steady in its presence.

فكرة الهوية

تجسّد الهوية فخامةً بحضورٍ متفرّد. تتداخل حروف شهم الإنجليزية SHM في رمزٍ واحد يجمع بين الخطوط الممتدة والانحناءات الدقيقة، ليمنح العلامة طابعها الخاص؛ هويةٌ متقنة في تفاصيلها، راسخة في حضورها.

المنطلق الإبداعي

الفخامة الحقيقية لا تحتاج إلى المبالغة؛ تظهر في دقّة التفاصيل وانسجام العناصر. وحين يحمل الاسم هيبته الخاصة، تصبح هويته توقيعًا لا يشبه سواه.

03 · The mark

A seal, not a logo

The monogram sits between a widely spaced Latin wordmark and an Arabic logotype, dated EST. 2026 like a house crest. On its own it can be cast in bronze, carved in stone or pressed in wax.

05 · Typography

Two scripts, one voice

The wordmark pairs widely tracked serif capitals with a refined Arabic logotype. The supporting type system extends that voice: a classical serif and a quiet geometric sans in Latin, matched by a calligraphic naskh and a clean modern sans in Arabic.

07 · Brand mark

Carved, not printed
