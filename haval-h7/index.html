Haval H7

Launch campaign · Discover the road, your way

ClientGWM · Haval
CampaignDiscover the road, your way
ScopeTeaser, reveal, OOH, social, launch event
RoleArt direction

Every driver has their own road.

The H7 arrived with a long list of strengths. We gathered them under one idea people could feel: the joy of discovery, and the freedom to choose your own path. The car is the means; the driver stays the hero.

01 · Tease

Show the details, hold the car

Close-up frames that revealed the car's details without ever showing it whole, pulling the audience step by step toward the moment it would appear.

02 · Reveal

Three ways to own the road

The H7 revealed through three core sides of the car, one frame each, so every placement sold a single reason to drive it.

03 · Launch

One night, one reveal

From welcoming the guests to the moment the car was unveiled, an experience built to keep the anticipation alive and give the launch its presence.

The road
is yours.

Teaser, reveal, outdoor, social and stage carried the same line, so the launch felt like one story told in many places.
