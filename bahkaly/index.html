Bahkaly

Brand guidelines · From port to progress · بحكلي

ClientBahkali Logistics
ProjectBrand guidelines
ScopeLogo, colour, type, pattern, applications
RoleArt direction

The arrow lives inside the k.

Bahkali has been moving goods across the region since 1987. The new mark turns the k into a road that leads forward and ends in an arrow, and that one line becomes the pattern, the routes and the fleet livery.

01 · The mark

One letter, one direction

A bold typographic logo with the arrow built into the k, plus variations, clear space, a grid and an app icon that works on its own.

02 · System

Deep green, moving fast

A deep green for trust, a bright green for speed and pure white for air. JUST Sans and Arvo in English, Almarai and Kufyan in Arabic, and a pattern of arrows.

From the desk to the highway
