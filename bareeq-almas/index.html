Bareeq Almas

Brand refresh & social media kit · Al Amoudi Group · بريق الماس

ClientAl Amoudi Group & Partners
ProductPlant-based margarine
ProjectBrand refresh & social media kit
RoleArt direction

A diamond for the kitchen.

Bareeq Almas means “diamond shine”. It is a plant-based margarine made to perform like butter for chefs, bakers and home kitchens. The diamond leads, royal blue holds the shelf, golden yellow tastes of butter, and an engraved illustration style gives every pack a sense of heritage.

01 · The mark

The diamond leads

A faceted diamond over a bilingual badge, with a safe space that protects it, black and white versions, stacked and horizontal lockups, and a set of emblems cut from the same stone.

Brand mark01 / 06
02 · Typography

Classic meets clean

A refined serif and a strong Arabic display face for headlines, with clean sans families for everything you read, so both languages share one premium voice.

03 · Colour

Blue, gold and butter

A diamond at the core and butter around it: royal blue, crimson and silver cut like facets, then golden amber, honey, sand and cerulean melting in to warm it up. The logo is approved on every one of them.

04 · Pattern

Cut from the same stone

The diamond repeats into patterns in yellow, crimson, cream and royal blue, for packs, bags, walls and backgrounds.

05 · Iconography

Drawn in one line

Clean monoline icons inspired by baking and natural ingredients, from croissants to whisks, in the same royal blue as the mark.

06 · Illustration

Engraved by hand

A fine engraving style drawn from traditional printmaking: kitchens, bakes, palms and farmland in royal blue line, giving the brand craft, care and a sense of heritage.

07 · Packaging

Blue on the shelf, gold inside

Tubs and blocks in royal blue, wrapped in silver foil, with the engraved farmland running across every face and a golden butter curl at the centre.

08 · Social

Made to be tasted

Close-up food on yellow and blue, a knife through a soft block, and a reveal from behind a red curtain, with the pack always in frame.

09 · In the world

From the aisle to the table

A supermarket stand and hanging signs, shelf strips, a bakery bag, a recipe card and breakfast on yellow: the same diamond, every step of the way.

Shine on
every shelf.
