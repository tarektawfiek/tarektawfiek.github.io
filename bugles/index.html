Bugles

World Cup 2026 activation · Crunch Fans League

ClientBugles
CampaignCrunch Fans League
ScopeIdentity, KVs, packaging, game mechanics, social, in-store, merch
RoleArt direction

Fans don't just watch. They compete.

Football fans predict, debate, track stats and celebrate every small win as if it were a goal. The campaign turns those match-day moments into a parallel game: every pack scanned is a point, every crunch is another win, while the teams compete on the pitch.

01 · Identity

A league of its own

A playful Arabic wordmark built for the stadium: a horn, a ball and a goal tucked into the letters, set in Bugles yellow, blue, green and orange.

01b · Game elements

Every win has a moment

A kit of reward moments built from the same playful language: the spin wheel, the confetti of Bugles cones, the "Mabrouk" burst and the champions' draw badge.

02 · Key visuals

Play with Bugles

Three key visuals for launch and support: the fan on his stadium sofa, the crew celebrating mid-pitch, and the scan-to-win car prize.

03 · Packaging

The pack is the ticket

A campaign ribbon runs down every flavour with the QR inside the pack, so the product itself becomes the entry to the game.

04 · The game

Scan, score, level up

No app to download: scan the QR, land on the mobile page, and every pack adds points toward instant rewards, weekly draws and the mega car draw.

  1. Step 1ScanBuy any pack, scan the QR inside.
  2. Step 2ScorePoints land in your wallet instantly.
  3. Step 3WinInstant rewards, weekly draws and a car at the end.
05 · Social

Built for the feed

A social system that keeps the energy of match day: big type, bold blue and yellow, and a QR on every post.

06 · In-store

The stadium in the aisle

A gaming zone, a football booth, a roll-and-win stand and shoot-score-win targets, each with a QR that pulls shoppers into the league.

07 · Giveaways

Wear the league

Kits and match balls in the campaign colours, so winners take the league home.

One crunch
at a time.

While the teams compete on the pitch, fans win their own way: from the pack to the phone, the aisle and the kit.
