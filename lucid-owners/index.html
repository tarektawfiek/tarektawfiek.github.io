Lucid Owners Club

Video series · Two owner-led concepts for Saudi Arabia

ClientLucid Motors · KSA
Seriesعلى طريقك · حكايات بين عالمين
ScopeSeries concepts, episodes, shot design, feature moments, b-roll, deliverables
RoleCreative concept & art direction

In the Gulf, a drive is never a straight line.

A simple ride turns into a chain of stops, conversations and side missions before it reaches anywhere. That movement is where life actually happens, so the owners, not the spec sheet, became the story.

Series 01 على طريقك On Your Way

A short-form, unscripted road series. Lucid owners take the host on spontaneous drives in their own car, talking about life, habits and what changed since they got a Lucid. It's not about where they're going, it's how they feel along the way.

Genuine

Ownership humanised through real, unscripted conversation.

Progressive

Lucid as part of lifestyle growth, not a product demo.

Relatable

A distinctly regional habit, so the story feels native, not imported.

02 · On the road

Four fixed angles, then the stops

Inside: a wide two-shot, owner close-up, host close-up and an over-the-shoulder from the back seat. Between them, swappable scene blocks keep the rhythm moving while the conversation flows.

Coffee stop

Grab cups, lean on the car, keep talking.

Charging stop

The car charges while the chat moves outside.

Scenic overlook

Corniche, skyline or a desert ridge.

Car wash

Hands-on pride of ownership.

Food run

Shawarma on the back seat, conversation on.

Family pickup

The car fills with new energy and laughter.

03 · Feature moments

Features you live, not read

No spec sheets. Each feature is woven into the drive so viewers discover it the way an owner does.

The start

No engine, no vibration. «ما حسّيت إنها مشت».

The silence

City noise outside, only their voices inside.

The sudden pull

Instant surge, a laugh, then back to the chat.

The glass roof

Sky passing overhead, light across their faces.

The sound moment

A favourite track fills the cabin on Surreal Sound.

The comfort cue

Massage seat on, a small smile, talk continues.

The frunk break

Cold drinks from the front trunk at a stop.

The app check

Range and charge at a glance, just a habit.

The navigation beat

The route loads before he finishes speaking.

The storage reveal

More space than the host expected.

Series 02 حكايات بين عالمين Stories Between Two Worlds

Not every journey is measured by distance. A cinematic series about real owners and the moments between who they are and who they're becoming, told between the Lucid Air and the Lucid Gravity.

Air

Ambition and forward motion.

Gravity

Presence and grounded decisions.

The drive

Where one world fades and another begins.

04 · The owners

A glimpse through their eyes

Each episode follows one owner: who they are, what they do and why they chose Lucid. Interview, cinematic b-roll and the owner's own footage blend into one honest film.

Human

Honest conversations about who they are and how they see life.

Layered

Interviews, b-roll and owner footage moving between reality and perspective.

Reflective

Lucid as a space for quiet clarity between destinations.

05 · Look & place

Golden light, three kinds of owner

Between luxury automotive film and honest human storytelling: rich golden light, shallow depth of field, and a camera that never tries too hard. Each owner gets a world that fits.

Adrenaline

Desert highways at golden hour, an empty Red Sea road.

Planet-conscious

Sustainable buildings and gardens in Riyadh or Jeddah.

Family-forward

Villa driveways, family compounds, a beachside chalet.

06 · B-roll

What words can't say

Three layers of b-roll: the owner's rituals with the car, the car alone as sculpture, and the owner's personal world in VHS-treated home footage.
