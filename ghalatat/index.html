Ghalatat

Brand identity & social media kit · Podcast by Rowad · غلطات

ClientRowad · Ghaltat Podcast
ProjectBrand identity & social media kit
ScopeLogo, system, templates, Season 2 launch
RoleArt direction

Every mistake has a story.

غلطات means mistakes: a podcast where founders tell the truth about what went wrong and what it taught them. The identity had to feel honest and grown-up, live on a phone screen, and give the stories room to lead.

01 · The mark

A signal you can hear

A logo-mark drawn like a broadcast signal, strong enough to stand alone as an app icon, and a rounded Arabic logotype set above GHALTAT PODCAST. White on deep green, with lime for the English line.

02 · System

Dark green, bright green, white

Three colours carry everything: a deep forest green, a teal, and a bright lime for the word PODCAST, with a gradient that fades from black into lime. Clearspace is set by the height of the letter غ.

Forest green#13413B
Teal#1C706C
Bright green#9BC05E

TypefaceNeo Sans Arabic

A modern Arabic typeface with a minimal look, simple and easy to read, in four weights that build hierarchy across every design.

03 · Usage

At home on any screen

Dark green, white and bright green first; black and photography when needed. Fine leaf arcs become the graphic element, on light paper or deep green.

06 · Colour & pattern

Deep green, lit in lime

Dark greens for trust and growth, lime and pale goldenrod for energy, a teal accent and a soft grey for balance. The flat background becomes a moving gradient with the brand's tree leaf, softly blurred, for depth and motion.

07 · Templates

One kit, every episode

Episode teasers with a guest silhouette, guest announcements, quote cards, text posts and minimal illustrations, all on the same gradient and leaf, with Rowad and Ghalatat locked up at the top.

08 · Season 2 teaser

Ghalatat 2 is back, stronger

«غلطات 2 راجع بقوة»: a static teaser, a video teaser and a website banner launch the season with harder questions, clearer answers and real stories from the people who lived them.
